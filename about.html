Who I am

Hello! My name is Natalia Gurbuz. I hold a Masters degree in Spanish and English Literature.

I have professional experience working with refugees in Canada, where I developed a strong passion for helping people navigate new environments.

Although my background is in language and communication, I have always been passionate about design.I love creating meaningful, user-centered experiences and putting myself into the users' shoes to understand their needs and perspectives.

Today, I am focused on combining my skills in communication and empathy to design solutions that make a real difference.

My Journey

My journey into design grew naturally from my desire to make things easier and more enjoyable for people.

With every project, I aim to create intuitive and beautiful designs that truly serve the user.

I am excited to continue growing as a designer and to take on new challenges where I can make a positive impact.
